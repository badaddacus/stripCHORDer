# stripCHORDer news feed
#
# RULES, ALL OF THEM:
#   - one message per line
#   - a line starting with # is ignored (use it for notes to yourself)
#   - a blank line is ignored
#   - only the FIRST 3 usable lines are shown, so put the important one at the top
#   - keep lines short: they scroll around a curve. Headline, not paragraph.
#     Anything past 120 characters gets cut off with a "..."
#
# OPTIONAL DATES. Put a date and a | in front of a line:
#   2026-12-01 | HOLIDAY SALE          shows from Dec 1st onward
#   2026-12-01..2026-12-26 | SALE      shows only between those two dates
# Dates mean it stops on its own and you don't have to remember to delete it.
#
# If you get a date wrong the line shows up with the date still on the front.
# That is deliberate - it's how you find out, rather than the message just
# never appearing.
